      Operating instructions

      You are shown a fragment of dialogue from a Doctor Who episode: anything from 2005 onwards, Ninth Doctor through Fifteenth.

      Identify the episode. Each incorrect attempt releases another fragment from the same episode, so it gets easier as you go.

      • You have 5 attempts and there are 5 fragments.
      • Fragments get more revealing each round.
      • From the fourth fragment onwards, the speaker is identified.
      • Incorrect attempts cost you nothing but a round, and typos cost you nothing at all, since titles are selected from the database.
      • Fail all five and you will be exterminated.

      A new episode every day at midnight, your local time. Missed one? The archive (📅 above) replays any earlier day, and will drill you on a random episode any time.

      Only the daily puzzle counts towards your record.

      Temporal archive

      Practise on any episode, or replay an earlier day. Neither touches your stats or streak.
